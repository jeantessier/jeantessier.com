When the entire blog is in a handwritten font, it is a little hard to read.
This
[article](https://martinfowler.com/articles/never-send-slides/build-your-slide-template.html)
by Sumeet Gayathri Moghe recommends using it parsimonously, mainly to draw the
reader's attention.  I scaled it back in this blog to the heading of each day's
entry and notes in the margin.
