I used [Jev](https://typesafe.ai/) to do some validation of user inputs to make
sure they don't enter garbage.  I could make a single call on the server-side
with a `noul` query for each field that needed validation, and receive a score
for each one about its probability of being a valid input.
[Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) gave me a working first
stab at structuring the `noul` structures to tell Jev what I was looking for.
